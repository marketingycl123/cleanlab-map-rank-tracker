How the numbers are calculated
  • Each bubble is the rank shown on Google Maps when searching from that exact spot (search-this-area on, mobile).
  • A listing that is not in the top 20 at a spot counts as rank 21 in the averages. Failed spots are ignored.
  • Share of voice is our own estimate: every listing scores 1 ÷ rank at each spot, and your share is your score divided by everyone's. It will not match other tools.
  • Average rank is taken over every spot on the grid, so it is high for everyone: a listing only ranks well near its own address. Compare yourself with the other rows, not with rank 1.
  • The coloured squares are an approximation: each spot is drawn as a square the size of the grid spacing around it, and the km² figures are spots × spacing². Google was only asked at the centre of each square.
  • Paid ads at the top of the list are not counted in the ranks.
